## Speed and machine notes

AMR streams through one long-lived ffmpeg per bitrate per loader worker (loader 829–1036 s/s); 4 workers for S2; validation cached on the GPU; closed phase on the GPU (`add_model.py`, `VA_DEVICE=cuda`, identical to CPU); `torch.compile` and HIP graph capture parked (no Triton on ROCm under Windows; capture aborts in hipBLASLt); priority opt-in (`VA_PRIORITY`), no measurable gain; under CPU load S2 loses 15–36 %, so keep measures off the CPU during training. Open: a batched GPU eval suite for the paired measures and the probe (closed phase done); profiling the training step needs ablation timing, since the ROCm profiler's GPU times are unreliable here. Machine notes: CPU boost on; CPPC core order 7, 8, 5, 4, 1, 6, 3, 2, then 11, 12, 13, 15, 10, 16, 9, 14 (HWiNFO numbering from 1); the main training thread runs on cores 6–7 (Windows numbering) and GPU use follows it.

## Training recipe experiments (next item 3)

Each runs on S2 and train_v2 against three same-seed baselines. The cheap screen is synthetic validation loss and F1–F3 error; anything that passes then gets the subset real-voice measures with 2–3 seeds. Optimiser and schedule choices tend to carry over to larger models. The best learning rate does not: it changes with model width.

| Experiment | Why |
|---|---|
| Shorter warmup (about 250–300 steps) | 1000 steps is 29 % of a 3.5k run |
| Larger batch (64), learning rate and steps adjusted | halves the per-step CPU cost per second of audio for small models |
| Learning-rate sweep (×0.5, ×2, ×3) | 1e-3 has never been tuned |
| Decay length about 20 % of the run (now 43 %) | the WSD literature finds about 20 % enough, so runs get cheaper |
| Muon for 2-D weights (GRU, linear), AdamW for the rest | built into torch 2.13; strong results on transformers, and relevant to the Conformer |
| Adan / AdanW | gains reported in vision and NLP papers; three betas to tune; not in torch, on PyPI |
| Schedule-free AdamW, retested with seeds | D-018 was a single-run comparison: better F1, worse f0 RPA |
| Cosine instead of linear warmup | usually small once the warmup length is right |
| `torch.compile`, channels-last | speed only; uncertain on ROCm under Windows |

- **Recorded noise and rooms in the channel model** (plan §5.6 listed them; never implemented, restored here 2026-10-23 at Hannah's question). Today every noise (white, pink, brown, hum, an amplitude-modulated noise "babble") and every RIR (exponential-decay model) is synthetic. Add, as extra `noise_kinds` and an RIR pool beside `synth_rir`: MUSAN's noise subset (OpenSLR 17, CC BY 4.0; ambient and environmental recordings); real RIRs and point-source noises from OpenSLR 28 (RIRS_NOISES; Apache 2.0 for the collection, its source databases' terms to check); café / crowd / traffic / street scenes from FSD50K, keeping only clips whose per-clip licence is CC0 or CC BY; and **real speech babble** mixed from our own pools (3–8 LibriTTS-R / Common Voice talkers), the hard realistic case for a voice analyser, labels staying the target voice's. Excluded on licence: DEMAND (CC BY-SA), WHAM! and ESC-50 (CC BY-NC). Matters most for real-world recordings (phone audio for outcomes work); the same pool serves denoising distillation (D-034). *Decided up front (Hannah, 2026-10-23)*: it goes in regardless of a clean-dev gate, which could only see its cost, since noisy real voices are what our data lacks most. Its test is therefore (1) no harm on clean dev (the usual guard-rails) and (2) improvement on **noisy copies of the dev gold sets**: gold audio mixed with a held-out slice of each noise / RIR source (reserved before any training use, never trained on) at fixed SNRs, evaluation only. The noise itself is varied per draw: random offset and length, gain, spectral tilt / EQ, speed perturbation (about ±10 %), two or three layered sources, and convolution with a real RIR so the noise shares the voice's room; the existing chain (filters, telephone band, codecs) then acts on voice and noise together. Downloaded 2026-10-23: MUSAN (OpenSLR 17), RIRS_NOISES (OpenSLR 28).
- **Voice-class heads on every model** (Hannah, 2026-10-23): age class (child / adult / unknown / null) and sex class (male / female / unknown / null), clip- or frame-level softmax; "null" for frames or clips with no voice, "unknown" for voices whose label is missing or ambiguous (teens, for age). The first heads with real-audio supervision: speaker metadata exists for LibriTTS-R, VCTK, speechocean762 and Samrómur (age, gender), Zeroth-Korean (sex), VocalSet (singer), MLS (gender) and partly Common Voice; synthetic clips take the generator's speaker group where it is recorded (D-022's population draws), otherwise "unknown".
